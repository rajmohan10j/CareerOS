## Purpose

Tracks every job application and its lifecycle.

## Statuses

- Evaluated
- Applied
- Responded
- Interview
- Offer
- Rejected
- Discarded
- Skip

## Data

- Company
- Role
- Job URL
- Resume used
- Cover letter used
- Status
- Notes
- Follow-up date
- Contacts
