# The CareerOS Manifesto

CareerOS exists to give every professional complete ownership of their career data, workflows, and AI assistance.

We believe careers should not depend on fragmented tools, vendor lock-in, or mandatory subscriptions. A single platform should enable users to manage resumes, applications, interviews, learning, networking, and long-term growth while keeping their data private and under their control.

## Our Commitments

- Open Source by Default
- Local First Execution
- Privacy Before Convenience
- Free Models as the Default Experience
- Human Approval for Critical Actions
- Transparent Development
- Documentation Before Implementation
- Modular, Extensible Architecture
- Community-Driven Governance
- Cross-Platform Accessibility

## What We Will Never Compromise

- User ownership of personal data.
- No mandatory paid AI services.
- No hidden telemetry enabled by default.
- No vendor lock-in.
- No undocumented architectural changes.
- Security and privacy take precedence over automation.

## Long-Term Vision

CareerOS aims to become the trusted open platform that accompanies a person throughout their professional life—from education to retirement—while remaining transparent, extensible, and freely available.
